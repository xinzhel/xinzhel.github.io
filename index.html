Xinzhe Li

Postdoctoral Research Fellow, RMIT University

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at RMIT University and hold a PhD in Information Technology from Deakin University. My research focuses on large language model (LLM) reasoning and planning via tree-structured search, and adaptive cross-trajectory agent memory, with first-author publications at ACL, TMLR, COLING, and IJCAI.

Research Interests

  • LLM tree search. A unified abstraction of policy, transition, and reward models for inference-time reasoning across language-grounded, tool-use, and environment-grounded tasks.
  • Adaptive branching. Deciding when to branch during search rather than expanding at every step, substantially reducing compute with little or no loss in accuracy.
  • Cross-trajectory agent memory. Transferring useful knowledge across reasoning attempts for tool-use agents, organized along the scope of transfer and the abstraction of content.
  • Closing the loop between inference and learning. Using search-generated trajectories to improve the underlying policy, and obtaining reliable reward signals without human annotation.
